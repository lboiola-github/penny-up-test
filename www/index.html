Welcome to Penny Up!

  • Tap the coin to earn money.
  • Buy businesses: they earn on their own, even when you are not tapping.
  • Catch the falling coins: they give rewards and bonuses.
  • Earn enough and you move to a better home.
  • While you are away, your piggy bank collects 20% of your income, up to 6 hours. Tap it when you come back.

Erase all progress?

Everything is erased and you start from zero: money, businesses, upgrades, homes, heirs, trophies and streak. This cannot be undone.

Inheritance

Retire and leave your fortune to your heirs. You start over in the Rented Room, but every heir makes all your income and taps 5% bigger, forever. The more you have earned in your whole life, the more heirs you get. Passing it on 1, 3 and 5 times earns trophies, and each time your home looks a little brighter.
